Exploring the Use of Symbolic Regression for Discovering Analytical Models in Small x,y Datasets: This paper introduces symbolic regression as a novel method for extracting interpretable analytical models from small x,y datasets. By leveraging evolutionary algorithms, symbolic regression can identify mathematical expressions that describe the relationship between variables, offering a transparent and flexible approach to model discovery. This method could reveal underlying functional forms that traditional statistical techniques might miss, providing a new avenue for understanding complex relationships in limited data scenarios.
